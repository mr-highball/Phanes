This is a collection of six songs I wrote many, many years ago as MIDIs in NoteWorthy Composer. To improve the sound quality, they have been converted to MP3 and FLAC using FL Studio. But for those who want access to the original MIDI files, I've added a third download option (the MIDI archive even includes a bonus piano song, "No One," that I wasn't able to convert to MP3).
